Safety first

Loops are generated automatically from open map data. They can be out of date or wrong, and they can't know about today's conditions.

  • Use your judgement. A path may be closed, private, flooded, unlit, under construction or unsafe for you. If something looks wrong, don't follow it.
  • Follow traffic laws and local rules. Pay attention to your surroundings, not the screen. Don't handle your phone while cycling.
  • Weather, light and "best time" suggestions are estimates. Check conditions yourself before you head out.
  • Know your limits. Outloop isn't a medical or fitness device. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise routine if you have health concerns.
  • Not for emergencies. Don't rely on Outloop for navigation where getting lost would be dangerous, such as in remote areas or in bad weather.
