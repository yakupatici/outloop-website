Who we are

Outloop ("Outloop", "we", "us") is an iPhone app that suggests walking and cycling loops that start and end where you are. This policy covers the Outloop app, its widgets and Live Activities, and this website. For personal data processed by our servers, we are the data controller.

We do not collect contacts, photos from your library, advertising identifiers, browsing history, or precise location in the background when you aren't recording.

Location

Outloop asks for location access while you're using the app. We use it to find loops that start near you, show where you are on the map and give turn-by-turn directions.

When you start recording a walk or ride, Outloop keeps receiving location updates until you stop, even if the screen is locked or you switch apps, so the recording isn't cut short. You'll see the location indicator and a Live Activity while this happens.

Your exact location is never shown to other people. When you share or export a route, start privacy moves the visible start and finish away from where you really were (you choose the distance, or turn it off, in You → Profile, privacy & your data).

To build loops and show conditions, the app sends the coordinates of the area or route, not your identity, to the services listed under Services we use.

Account and backup

Signing in

You can use Outloop with an account created with Sign in with Apple, Google or an email and password. We receive your email address (or Apple's private relay address if you choose to hide yours), your name if you share it, and a unique account ID. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.

Backup is off until you turn it on

With Back up to your account switched on (in the You tab), Outloop stores a copy of your saved loops, recorded walks and rides including their GPS tracks, drafts, collections, and your display name, username and start-privacy setting, so they can be restored when you sign in on another iPhone.

So that something you deleted on one phone doesn't come back from another, the backup also keeps a short list of what you deleted and when, for up to 180 days. This list contains identifiers only, not the deleted content.

You can turn backup off at any time. Backups are protected so that only your signed-in account can read them.

Deleting your account

Go to the You tab → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and your backup from our servers. If you signed in with Apple, we also ask Apple to revoke Outloop's access to your Apple ID. Data stored only on your phone stays there until you erase it (You → Profile, privacy & your data → Hold to delete all data on this device) or delete the app.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Outloop:

  • reads only today's step count, to suggest a loop that closes the gap to your daily goal;
  • writes your finished walks and rides to Apple Health as workouts, with their route, so they count towards your Activity rings.

Health data is used only on your iPhone. It's never sent to our servers, never used for advertising or marketing, and never sold or shared with anyone. You can change what Outloop can read and write at any time in the Health app or in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

To protect the free services we depend on from abuse, our gateway keeps a one-way hash of the IP address of each request for rate limiting. The hash can't be turned back into your address, and it's deleted within 24 hours. Search results for map areas are cached without any information about who asked.

Crash reports

When the app crashes or freezes, iOS gives Outloop a diagnostic report (through Apple's MetricKit). Outloop sends that report to us together with the app version and iOS version. These reports are not linked to your account, don't include your location, and are used only to fix problems. If you've turned off "Share With App Developers" in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, iOS doesn't provide these reports.

Purchases

Outloop Plus is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. The app checks your subscription status with Apple on your device.

Sharing and export

Nothing you record is shared with other people unless you share it yourself, for example by sending a route, exporting a GPX file or sharing a story image. When you share, the recipient gets what you send.

You → Profile, privacy & your data → Export my data creates a file with your saved loops, activity, private routes and collections. It includes exact GPS tracks, so only share it with people you trust.

We don't sell or rent personal data, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose information if the law requires it, and we'll tell you when we're allowed to.

How long we keep it

  • Account details: until you delete your account.
  • Backup: until you delete your account. Each new backup replaces the previous one. The list of deleted items is kept for up to 180 days.
  • Crash reports: only as long as needed to fix the problem, and no longer than 12 months.
  • Hashed IP addresses for rate limiting: up to 24 hours.
  • Data on your iPhone: until you erase it in the app or delete the app.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the EU and UK GDPR and Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK), you have the right to access, correct, export and delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time.

Most of this you can do yourself in the app: export your data, turn backup off, erase data on your phone or delete your account. For anything else, contact us and we'll respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

We process your data to provide the app you asked for (performance of a contract), on the basis of your consent for backup, location and Health access, and on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and secure for crash reports and rate limiting.

International transfers

Our servers, operated by Supabase, and some of the providers above may be located outside your country. When data is transferred abroad, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, and on your explicit consent when you turn on backup.

Children

Outloop isn't directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we don't knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use your data, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
