How can we help?

Quick answers to the questions we hear most. If yours isn't here, write to us. A real person reads every message.

Loops and routes

How does Outloop find a loop?

It looks at real footpaths, parks, cycle paths and places near you, then builds loops of the length you asked for that start and end where you are. Your mood choices decide what the loop leans towards: cafés, water, greenery or quiet streets.

A loop goes somewhere I can't walk. What should I do?

Don't follow it, and pick another loop. Route data comes from OpenStreetMap, which is edited by volunteers and is sometimes out of date. You can help everyone by correcting it at openstreetmap.org.

No loops show up near me.

Check that Outloop has location access (Settings → Outloop → Location → While Using the App) and that you're online. In places with few mapped paths, try a longer time or the other activity.

Can I use Outloop offline?

Yes. Download a loop from its detail page before you go and it works without signal, map included. Free accounts can keep one loop offline; Plus has no limit.

Does recording work with the screen off?

Yes. Once you start recording, Outloop keeps tracking until you stop, with a Live Activity on your Lock Screen. If the app is ever closed mid-walk, it offers to recover the recording next time you open it.

Outloop Plus

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Outloop, and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep Plus until the end of the period you've paid for.

I bought Plus but the app doesn't show it.

Open the You tab → Outloop Plus and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Account and privacy

Do I need an account?

An account lets you back up your loops and walks and restore them on a new iPhone. Backup is off until you turn it on in the You tab.

How do I move my loops to a new iPhone?

On your old iPhone, turn on Back up to your account in the You tab and wait for "Backed up". Sign in with the same account on the new iPhone and your loops come back.

How do I delete my account?

Open the You tab and tap Delete account. Your account and backup are deleted from our servers. If you signed in with Apple, you'll be asked to confirm with Apple once more.

How do I export or erase my data?

Open You → Profile, privacy & your data. Export my data creates a file with everything you've saved and recorded. Hold to delete all data on this device erases it from your iPhone.

Who can see where I walk?

Nobody, unless you share a route yourself. Even then, start privacy hides where you really started and finished. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Still stuck?

Tell us what happened and which iPhone and iOS version you're using. We usually reply within two working days.
